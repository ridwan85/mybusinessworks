## Quick verdict

You know that moment when the couch is cold, but heating the whole house feels like too much for one person? You want warmth on you, not a hotter room you are not sitting in. A heated throw is meant for that lap, not the whole house.

The <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0BZR6YBHK?tag=dailyvirald06-20" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">Bedsure heated throw</a> is a 50 by 60 inch blanket, and this review is the grey one. It has a soft side and a fuzzy side, and you plug it in when you want heat.


## What it does

* **Heat on you.** There are six levels, a controller, and timers at 1, 2, 4, 6, 8, or 10 hours. The claim is longer heating wires spread the warmth.

* **Preheat.** The claim is you can preheat, then drop to a level you can stay with, but the exact minutes depend on the room.

* **Two fabrics.** One side is flannel and the other is sherpa, which is the fuzzy side. The fabric-weight number on the page just means how thick the cloth is. You can also use it unplugged, because it is still a normal throw.

* **100 watts.** It plugs into a normal 120 volt outlet. The claim is it uses less power than heating a room with a space heater, but it still needs a plug nearby.

* **Safety marks.** It is ETL and FCC certified, and the default auto shutoff is 8 hours so it can help prevent overheating. If you want longer, you restart it.

* **Washing.** They claim you can wash it in a machine, but unplug the controller first and follow the tag in the box.

## Who it's for

It fits people who are always cold on the couch, at a desk, or in bed as an extra layer, and it is an easy gift for a housewarming, a birthday, or a family visit.

Skip it if you need one blanket to cover a whole mattress, because this is a throw.

## The good parts

* Six heat levels and several timers.
* You can preheat, then settle on a steadier level.
* It stays soft even when it is off.
* The claim is it uses less power than heating a whole room.
* It shuts off after 8 hours, and it is an easy gift to understand.

## Honest limits

* At 50 by 60 inches it covers a person, not a bed.
* You need an outlet and a place for the controller.
* The 8-hour shutoff will stop a very long night, so you restart it if you want more.

## FAQ

### Can I wash it?

Yes. They claim a machine wash is fine, but disconnect the controller first and read the tag that comes with it.

### How fast does it preheat?

The claim is the preheat gets it toasty quickly, but the room temperature changes that.

### Can I fall asleep with it on?

Yes. It has an 8-hour auto shutoff, so read the manual before you sleep with it on.

### Can I use it with the heat off?

Yes. Flannel is on one side and sherpa is on the other, so it is still a throw. The heated version is the <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0BZR6YBHK?tag=dailyvirald06-20" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">Bedsure heated throw</a>.

## Bottom line

This throw is for a cold lap, not a cold house, because you get six levels, a timer, and a soft blanket when it is off.

Skip it if you need a full-bed blanket. If the room itself is cold, a space heater is the other tool.
