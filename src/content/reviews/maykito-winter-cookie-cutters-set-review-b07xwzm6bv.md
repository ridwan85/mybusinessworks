## Quick verdict

The dough is rolled out. The one cutter you need is missing. Warm dough also sticks, so the shape comes out soft even when you find a cutter. A full set of metal shapes is meant to give you a clean outline, but you still ice the cookie yourself.

The <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07XWZM6BV?tag=dailyvirald06-20" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">Maykito cookie cutter set</a> is 12 stainless shapes for winter baking at home.


## What it does

* **Twelve metal shapes.** They are stainless steel, in a box, for a winter tray. Think family baking and gift cookies, not a shop display.

* **A cleaner cut.** Metal holds its edge better than thin plastic that bends, but dough thickness and a cold rest still matter, because warm sticky dough fights every cutter.

* **Hand wash.** Dry them after, because wet cutters left in a pile can rust over time. That is normal for bare steel.

* **Not a full bake kit.** There are no pans, no icing, and no tips, so you bring those.

## Who it's for

It fits home bakers who already have dough and want a full set of shapes, and it fits a gift for someone who bakes with kids.

Skip it if you need giant display cookies or deep molds, or if you only use a dishwasher and never dry tools.

## The good parts

* Twelve shapes in one box.

## Honest limits

* Hand wash and dry them, because a wet pile can rust.
* Chill the dough if the shapes tear.
* Icing skill makes the cookie pretty, and the cutter only makes the outline.
* These are home sizes, not oversized bakery cutters.

## FAQ

### Are these plastic?

No. They are stainless steel, so dry them and do not let them sit wet.

### Will the shapes come out clean?

Yes, if the dough is cold and firm. Soft dough sticks and tears, and the cutter cannot fix warm dough.

### Do they include icing?

No. You get cutters, and you bring the dough and the icing. See the <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07XWZM6BV?tag=dailyvirald06-20" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">Maykito cookie cutter set</a> if you want the shape list on Amazon.

### Can kids use them?

Yes, with a grown-up nearby. Metal edges are for dough, not for rough play, because this is a baking tool.

## Bottom line

If winter cookies start with the right shapes, this set is the simple buy, because you get twelve stainless cutters in one box.

Skip it if you need molds or huge cookies. Dry the metal after you wash it, and the pretty icing is still your job.
