## Who It's For

This eye massager is designed for individuals who experience daily eye strain and head tension from heavy computer use. If you work long hours at a desk, spend your evenings staring at smartphones or monitors, or frequently travel by air and need a way to unwind on flights, this device fits right into your routine.
