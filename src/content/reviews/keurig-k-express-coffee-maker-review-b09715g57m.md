## Quick verdict

The keys are in your hand, the coffee is not ready, and a full pot feels too slow. One button for one cup is the point when you do not want to wait on a carafe.

The <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B09715G57M?tag=dailyvirald06-20" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">Keurig K-Express</a> makes one cup from a K-Cup pod, and this review is the black model. The claim is a cup is ready in minutes. It is simple on purpose, with no schedule and no big screen.

## What it does

* **Three sizes.** Press a button for 8, 10, or 12 oz.

* **A 42 oz tank.** They claim that is about four small cups before you refill, and the tank comes off so you can fill it at the sink.

* **Strong brew.** One setting makes a bolder cup, but it is still the same pod, not a different coffee.

* **A travel mug.** Take the drip tray off and a mug up to 7.4 inches tall can fit. The tray also catches drips.

* **It shuts off.** Five minutes after the last cup, it turns off, and that is how it saves a little power.

* **Your own grounds.** It works with the My K-Cup reusable filter, but that filter is sold separately.

## Who it's for

It fits one person, a small apartment, a dorm, or an office corner, and it fits a house where each person wants a different size.

It is a weak fit if you fill thermoses for a crowd every morning.

## The good parts

* One cup without a full pot.
* You can pick 8, 10, or 12 oz.
* The tank lifts off.
* Strong brew when you want it bolder.
* It turns off after 5 minutes, and there is room for a tall mug.

## Honest limits

* After about four small cups you refill, so a busy house will do that often.
* There is no programmable timer, so it will not start before you wake up.
* Ground coffee needs the My K-Cup, which is sold separately.
* Pods are the normal path, so budget for them.

## FAQ

### How many cups does the tank hold?

The tank is 42 oz. They claim that is up to four cups if each cup is 8 oz, so bigger cups mean fewer fills.

### Can I use ground coffee?

Yes, with the My K-Cup reusable filter, but it is sold separately. Most people use pods.

### Will a travel mug fit?

Yes, if you remove the drip tray. They claim mugs up to 7.4 inches tall fit. Check your mug against the <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B09715G57M?tag=dailyvirald06-20" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">Keurig K-Express</a> page if it is extra tall.

### When does it turn off?

Five minutes after the last brew, so you do not have to remember a switch.

## Bottom line

The K-Express is a fast one-cup maker, so you pick a size, press the button, and go.

Skip it if you want a pot for the whole house, or if you need coffee waiting when the alarm rings. A kettle is the other path if you make tea or pour-over.
