## Quick Verdict

The Ninja IG651 Foodi Smart XL Pro 7-in-1 Indoor Grill/Griddle Combo is a remarkably versatile kitchen appliance designed for home cooks who want outdoor BBQ flavors and multiple cooking methods without stepping outside. By combining a 500-degree powered grill grate, a 12-inch flat top BBQ griddle, and an air fryer into a single footprint, it takes the hassle out of preparing complex meals. While it has a sizable countertop footprint, the sheer flexibility of having seven functions—including roast, bake, broil, and dehydrate—makes it a strong contender for busy households.

## Key Features

- **Ninja's Most Powerful Grill:** Powered by a 500°F independently powered grill grate that creates direct, edge-to-edge high-heat searing for better char-grilled results compared to previous FG500-series models.
- **12-Inch BBQ Griddle:** A flat-top BBQ griddle that cooks foods traditional grills cannot, interlocking with the grate to harness direct high heat for fast, even cooking without hot or cold spots.
- **Hood Up or Hood Down Cooking:** Keep the hood down for cyclonic air to achieve fast, even, high-heat cooking, surround searing, crisping, and melting. Keep the hood up for intense high-heat searing and direct bottom heat.
- **Smart Cook System Plus Thermometer:** The Foodi Smart Thermometer features 4 smart protein settings and 9 customizable doneness levels to achieve the exact doneness you want from rare to well-done at the touch of a button.
- **7-in-1 Versatility:** Features Grill, BBQ Griddle, Air Crisp, Roast, Bake, Broil, and Dehydrate functions enabled by a wide temperature range.
- **XL Family-Sized Capacity:** The grill grate fits up to 6 steaks, mains, and sides simultaneously, while the flat top griddle accommodates a whole platter of nachos, 4 sandwiches, or 6 pancakes.
- **Smoke Control System:** Combines chef-recommended grilling practices, a temperature-controlled grill grate, a splatter shield, and a cool-air zone to reduce smoke and keep it out of the kitchen.
- **Easy-to-Clean Parts:** Includes a PTFE/PFOA-free, nonstick ceramic-coated powered grill grate, crisper basket, and BBQ griddle plate that are dishwasher safe.

## Who It's For

This indoor grill and griddle combo is tailored for busy home cooks, families, and apartment dwellers who love the taste of grilled and griddled food but lack outdoor space or want to avoid weather limitations. If you frequently find yourself cooking multiple batches of food or dirtying several pans to make breakfast, lunch, and dinner, the extra-large capacity and multi-functional design will streamline your kitchen routine. It is also an ideal choice for anyone who struggles with meat temperatures, as the built-in smart thermometer handles the monitoring for you.

## FAQ

### Can I use the grill and the griddle at the same time?
No, the grill grate and the 12-inch flat top BBQ griddle interchange in the base, allowing you to use one cooking surface at a time depending on your recipe.

### How does the smoke control system work?
It utilizes a combination of chef-recommended grilling practices, a temperature-controlled grill grate, a splatter shield, and a cool-air zone to significantly reduce smoke production during indoor cooking.

### Are the cooking plates dishwasher safe?
Yes, the PTFE/PFOA-free, nonstick ceramic-coated powered grill grate, crisper basket, and BBQ griddle plate are all dishwasher safe for easy cleaning.

### What is included in the box?
The package includes the Ninja Foodi Smart XL Pro Griddle & Grill base, a 9" x 12" Grill Grate, a 12" Flat Top BBQ Griddle, a 4-qt. Crisper Basket, the Foodi Smart Thermometer, and a Quick Start Guide.

For households looking to expand their indoor cooking capabilities with reliable, family-sized capacity, this 7-in-1 combo is a worthwhile kitchen upgrade.
