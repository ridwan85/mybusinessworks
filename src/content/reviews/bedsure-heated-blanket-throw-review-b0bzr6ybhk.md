## Quick verdict

The couch is cold. Heating the whole house feels like too much.

The <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0BZR6YBHK?tag=dailyvirald06-20" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">Bedsure heated throw</a> is a 50 by 60 inch blanket. This review is the grey one. It has a soft side and a fuzzy side. You plug it in for heat. This page did not sleep under it.

## What it does

* **Heat on you.** Six levels. A controller. Timers at 1, 2, 4, 6, 8, or 10 hours. Bedsure says longer heating wires spread the warmth.

* **Preheat.** The listing says you can preheat, then drop to a level you can stay with. Exact minutes depend on the room. This page did not time it.

* **Two fabrics.** A flannel side and a sherpa side. Sherpa is the fuzzy side. GSM on the listing is just fabric weight. You can also use it unplugged as a normal throw.

* **100 watts.** It plugs into a normal 120 volt outlet. Bedsure says that uses less power than heating a room with a space heater. It still needs a plug nearby.

* **Safety marks.** ETL and FCC certified. The default auto shutoff is 8 hours. That is to help prevent overheating. If you want longer, you restart it.

* **Washing.** The listing says it is machine-care friendly. Unplug the controller first. Follow the tag in the box.

## Who it's for

It fits people who are always cold on the couch, at a desk, or in bed as an extra layer. It is a gift for a housewarming, a birthday, or a family visit.

Skip it if you need one blanket to cover a whole mattress. This is a throw.

## The good parts

* Six heat levels and several timers.
* Preheat, then a steadier level.
* Soft even when it is off.
* Less power than heating a whole room, per the listing.
* Auto shutoff at 8 hours.
* A gift that is easy to understand.

## Honest limits

* 50 by 60 inches covers a person, not a bed.
* You need an outlet and a place for the controller.
* The 8-hour shutoff will stop a very long night. You restart it if you want more.
* This page did not measure heat-up time.

## FAQ

### Can I wash it?

The listing says machine care is fine. Disconnect the controller first. Read the tag that comes with it.

### How fast does it preheat?

Bedsure says the preheat gets it toasty quickly. The room temperature changes that. This page did not time it.

### Can I fall asleep with it on?

It has an 8-hour auto shutoff and ETL and FCC marks. That is the safety design. It is not a promise for every sleeper. Read the manual.

### Can I use it with the heat off?

Yes. Flannel on one side, sherpa on the other. It is still a throw. The heated version is the <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0BZR6YBHK?tag=dailyvirald06-20" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">Bedsure heated throw</a>.

## Bottom line

This throw is for a cold lap, not a cold house. Six levels. A timer. A soft blanket when it is off.

Skip it if you need a full-bed blanket. If the room itself is cold, a space heater is the other tool.
