## Quick verdict

You know that moment when your kid's bag is already packed, and you still have to squeeze in one more heavy book? A tablet seems easier, until they take it outside and the screen washes out in the sun. Then the games start calling, and the story gets left behind. This little Kindle is meant to fix all three.

The <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0GV5RHY69?tag=dailyvirald06-20" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">Amazon Kids Kindle Colorsoft</a> is a 7-inch color reader for ages 6 to 12, and the bundle includes a cover plus one year of Kids+.


## What it does

* **Color that still reads outside.** The claim is the screen looks more like paper and stays readable in bright sun, so comics and illustrated books are the reason to pick color.

* **Reading, not a toy tablet.** There are no apps, videos, games, or notifications, so the device stays on the story.

* **What comes in the box.** You get the reader, a protective case, and 1 year of Kids+. That year includes thousands of kids' books and comics, but after the year it renews at the current rate unless you cancel, so read that line before you buy.

* **A light you can warm.** You can shift the front light from white to warm amber, which helps at bedtime.

* **Water.** The claim is it is waterproof for the pool, the beach, or the bath, but you still need to follow their care notes.

* **Battery and space.** The claim is up to 8 weeks, and that depends on how you use it. 16GB holds a lot of text, but color comics take more space than plain novels.

## Who it's for

It fits parents of kids about 6 to 12 who want reading, not another game device, and it also fits travel when a stack of graphic novels is too much to carry.

Skip it if your child wants a tablet full of apps, or if you will not watch the renewal date.
