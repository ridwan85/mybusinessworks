## Quick verdict

You get home late. The oven is still cold. Nobody wants to stand over a pan. The wait is the preheat, not the food. Hot air can crisp dinner without that long heat-up, which is the point on a weeknight.

The <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0H87WV6N4?tag=dailyvirald06-20" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">Ninja Air Fryer Pro 5 Qt</a> blows 400°F air around the food. The claim is hot, crispy results with little or no oil. This color is Bone and Champagne. It is meant for frozen food, chicken, vegetables, and quick sides, not every dish.


## What it does

* **Hot air, not a pot of oil.** Ninja calls it Air Crisp. They claim the air gets as hot as 400°F and wraps the food.

* **Five jobs.** You can air fry, bake, roast, reheat, and dehydrate.

* **A 5-quart basket.** It holds up to 4 pounds of fries or 5 pounds of chicken wings, and there is a crisper plate inside.

* **Less oil.** The claim is up to 75% less fat than deep-fried fries, and they compared it with fries fried in oil.

* **A smaller footprint.** They claim this shape saves counter space without shrinking the basket, but you should still measure your counter.

* **Cleanup.** The basket and plate are nonstick, so use a soft sponge and do not scratch the coat.

## Who it's for

It fits busy people, couples, and small families, and it fits you if frozen food and roasted chicken are normal meals.

A huge dinner for a crowd may need two batches. If you love a real deep fryer, the texture will feel different.

## The good parts

* Hot air, so you are not waiting on a cold oven.
* Five cook modes in one basket.
* Room for a family tray of fries or wings.
* Nonstick parts that wash faster than a greasy pan.
* A color that is not plain black.

## Honest limits

* Big families may cook in batches.
* Measure the counter depth before you buy.
* Nonstick coats hate rough scrubbers.
* The 75% less fat line is a test claim, not a promise for every recipe.

## FAQ

### What fits in the 5-quart basket?

It holds up to 4 pounds of fries or 5 pounds of chicken wings, and you can also roast vegetables, bake, or dehydrate. Check the <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0H87WV6N4?tag=dailyvirald06-20" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">Ninja Air Fryer Pro</a> for the exact model.

### Is the basket hard to clean?

No. The basket and plate are nonstick, so most bits come off with warm soapy water and a soft sponge.

### Do I have to use oil?

No. They claim the hot air crisps food with little or no oil, and a light spray is optional. It is not the same as deep frying.

### Will it crowd my counter?

No. They claim the shape saves space, but measure your counter first.

## Bottom line

This 5-quart Ninja is for nights when the oven feels too slow, because one basket covers five modes and crisps food with less oil.

Skip it if you feed a crowd every night, or if you want true deep-fried food. For stews and beans, a pressure cooker is the other tool.
