## Quick verdict

You step out of the shower and the towel is cold. That is a rough start on a winter morning, because the bathroom air cools the towel while you wash. Cranking the heat for the whole house is a big fix for one towel. A bucket that warms the towel is the smaller fix.

The <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0C7KQTXV1?tag=dailyvirald06-20" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">SAMEAT towel warmer</a> is a tall white bucket. The wood handle is on the lid, and the buttons sit on the rim of the body. You add a dry towel, close the lid, and it turns itself off.

## What it does

* **A tall white bucket.** The body is white, the handle is wood, and the controls are on the top rim of the body, not on the lid. This one is model TW-ST1912. It holds up to two 40 by 70 inch towels. A robe, a blanket, or pajamas can go in too, if the fabric can take the heat.

* **It turns itself off.** You get 30 minutes, 1 hour, 2 hours, or 4 hours. Then it stops. It starts on 30 minutes, and the timer light stays off during that first run, so a dark light does not mean it is off.

* **Power.** It is 450 watts, on 100 to 120 volts, and it can draw up to 5 amps. Do not overload the outlet.

* **How warm it gets.** The claim is that the towel heats all the way through, and that it heats up fast. There is no minute count for that heat-up.

* **What can go inside.** Dry cotton is the safe fabric. Skip wet towels, chemical fiber, and anything that cannot take heat. When the hot-surface light is on, do not touch the inside.

* **The size lines do not match.** One line is 21.9 by 13.4 by 13.3 inches. The box line is 19.75 by 12.5 by 12.5 inches, and the weight is 9.18 pounds. Measure the spot if your counter is tight. You also get a manual in the box. It is ETL certified, with a 12-month warranty and a 30-day return.

## Who it's for

This is for someone who wants a warm towel after a shower and does not want to remodel the bathroom. It also works as a winter gift, because there is no clothing size to guess.

Skip it if the towel is wet, or if the real problem is a cold room.

## Honest limits

* It is not a dryer. A wet towel does not belong inside.
* It will not warm the bathroom air.
* The first timer light stays dark, so you can think it is off when it is not.
* The two size lines do not agree, so measure before you buy.
* Do not touch the inside while the hot light is on.

## FAQ

### Can it dry a wet towel?

No. It is for dry cotton, not for a towel that is still damp.

### Does it turn itself off?

Yes. You pick 30 minutes, 1 hour, 2 hours, or 4 hours, and then it stops. It starts on 30 minutes.

### Will it warm the bathroom?

No. It warms what is inside the bucket. For the air in the room, you want a heater. The <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0C7KQTXV1?tag=dailyvirald06-20" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">SAMEAT towel warmer</a> is the towel, not the room.

### Can I touch the inside while it is on?

No. Wait until the hot-surface light is off.
