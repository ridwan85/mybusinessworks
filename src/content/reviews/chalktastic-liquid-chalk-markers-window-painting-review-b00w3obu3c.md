## Quick verdict

You want color on the window. You do not want paint that stays till spring.

The <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B00W3OBU3C?tag=dailyvirald06-20" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">Chalktastic liquid chalk markers</a> are washable pens for glass and other smooth surfaces. The set is for temporary art and signs. This page did not draw a window with them.

## What it does

* **Color that is meant to wipe off.** Use it on clean glass and other non-porous surfaces. Porous boards may not wipe the same way.

* **Two tip sizes.** The tips reverse, so you can make a fine line or a broader stroke.

* **More than one pass.** Bold color on clear glass can need a second coat. That is normal for this kind of ink.

* **Indoor first.** Rain, freeze, and long outdoor time can fade the art. This is not vinyl that stays up for weeks.

* **Test a corner.** Tinted film and delicate finishes deserve a small test before you cover them.

## Who it's for

It fits families who want winter window art and kid crafts. It fits a temporary sign on glass.

Skip it for outdoor lettering that must survive weather. Skip it if you only use dusty stick chalk on a real chalkboard.

## FAQ

### Will it come off the window?

That is the point on smooth glass. Clean the glass first. Wipe when you are done. A porous surface may not release the same way.

### Is it paint?

No. It is liquid chalk. It is for temporary art, not a permanent window job.

### Can it stay outside in the rain?

Do not count on it. Indoor windows are the easy job. Weather means touch-ups. Read the <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B00W3OBU3C?tag=dailyvirald06-20" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">Chalktastic markers</a> page for the set size you are buying.

### Can kids use them?

Yes, for window art and crafts, with a grown-up nearby. They are washable, not a free pass to draw on every wall.

## Bottom line

If you want winter color on glass without a forever mess, these markers are the simple tool.

Skip them for rain-proof signs or a real chalkboard. For a kitchen project instead, cookie cutters are the other winter activity.
