## Quick verdict

You know that moment when you are late and breakfast is still fruit in the fridge? A big blender means a cord, a pour, and a wash, and that is three extra steps you do not have. A cup you blend in and then drink from is meant to skip them.

The <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0C2FF4JD1?tag=dailyvirald06-20" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">Ninja Blast</a> is a cordless blender with an 18 oz cup. You blend in the cup, then drink from it. This page covers the black one, so buy the color you will carry, not a spare for every bag.


## What it does

* **No cord while you blend.** The base is a 7.4V rechargeable motor, and you charge it with USB-C. The cable is included.

* **One 18 oz cup.** It is for smoothies, protein shakes, and frozen drinks. The cup is ribbed so the mix pulls down toward the blades, and the blades are stainless steel. Ninja calls them BlastBlade.

* **A lid for the road.** They claim the sip lid seals when it is locked, and there is a carry handle.

* **Two buttons.** Power and blend are separate, so it is less likely to start in a bag.

* **Cleanup.** The lid and cup are BPA-free, which means no harmful plastic chemical called BPA. They claim the lid and cup are dishwasher safe, or you can add water and a drop of soap for a 30-second clean.

* **In the box.** You get the motor base, the cup, a sip lid with a handle, a USB-C cable, a quick start, a 5-recipe guide, and a booklet.

## Who it's for

It fits rushed mornings, gym bags, and trips, and it also fits a dorm, a small kitchen, or an RV.

It is one serving, so it is not a pitcher for a family breakfast.

## The good parts

* Cordless, with USB-C charging.
* You blend and drink from the same 18 oz cup.
* They claim the locked lid does not leak.
* A 30-second self-clean, or the dishwasher.
* Separate buttons so it is less likely to start in a bag.

## Honest limits

* 18 oz is one person, not a crowd.
* You have to keep the battery charged.
* Very thick mixes need enough liquid.
* A perfectly smooth drink is a claim, so the liquid still matters.

## FAQ

### Can it crush ice?

Yes. They claim the steel blades and ribbed cup can crush ice and frozen fruit, but use enough liquid.

### How do I clean it?

Add water and a drop of dish soap for a 30-second spin, or wash the BPA-free lid and cup in the dishwasher.

### Will it leak in a bag?

No. They claim the lid seals when it is locked, so lock it before you pack it. See the <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0C2FF4JD1?tag=dailyvirald06-20" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">Ninja Blast</a> page for the cup you are buying.

### How do you charge it?

The base charges with the included USB-C cable, but you still need a USB power source.

## Bottom line

The Ninja Blast is for one drink, fast, away from the wall, so you blend, sip, and go.

Skip it if you need a pitcher, or if you will not charge it. A counter blender is the other tool when the batch is big.
