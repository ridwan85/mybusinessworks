## Quick verdict

The sponge is wet. The soap is slimy. The brush is in the same puddle.

The <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0CJ4WZXQF?tag=dailyvirald06-20" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">Cisily sink caddy</a> gives those three things their own spots. The tray slopes so water can run off. It is stainless steel.

## What it does

* **Three spots.** Sponge, brush, and soap. They stop stacking in one wet pile.

* **A sloped tray.** They call it a self-drain. Water is meant to leave the sponge area. You still wipe the tray sometimes. Drain does not mean never clean it.

* **Stainless steel.** They claim it resists rust and warping, and it rinses under the tap. "Resists" is not a forever promise.

* **No install.** Open the box and set it on the counter or the sink edge. No drill. No sticky pad.

* **Not only the kitchen.** They also show it in a bathroom for soap and a brush.

## Who it's for

It fits a small kitchen where the sink is the messy spot. It fits renters who cannot remodel. It is an easy housewarming gift.

Skip it if you love a hanging suction caddy. This one sits. Skip it if you need a rack for plates.

## The good parts

* A spot each for sponge, brush, and soap.
* A slope so water is not stuck under the sponge.
* Stainless steel you can rinse.
* No tools to set it up.
* Small enough for a tight counter.

## Honest limits

* It holds sink tools, not a drawer of gadgets.
* You still clean the tray. Less often is the goal, not never.
* Check faucet height and sink depth so it actually fits.
* A sticky under-cabinet holder is a different idea.

## FAQ

### Does it drain by itself?

Yes. The tray is sloped so water can move away from the sponge. You still rinse it.

### Will it rust?

No, not if you rinse it. They claim the stainless steel resists rust. That is not a forever promise.

### Do I have to install it?

No. Set it down and load it. They claim the <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0CJ4WZXQF?tag=dailyvirald06-20" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">Cisily sink caddy</a> is ready out of the box.

### Can it live in the bathroom?

Yes. They also show it with hand soap and a brush, not only dish tools.

## Bottom line

If the sink is the messiest square in the kitchen, this caddy is a small fix. Three spots. A drain. No install.

It will not dry your plates. It will not organize the whole counter. For the sponge you touch every day, that is the job.
