## Quick verdict

You walk in tired. The food is in the fridge. Dinner still feels an hour away. Standing at the stove is the part that makes the night longer. One pot that can cook under pressure is meant to shorten that wait.

The <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B00FLYWNYQ?tag=dailyvirald06-20" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">Instant Pot Duo 6 Qt</a> is one electric pot with seven jobs: pressure cooker, slow cooker, rice cooker, steamer, sauté pan, yogurt maker, and warmer. The claim is pressure cooking can be up to 70% faster than older methods.


## What it does

* **Seven jobs in one pot.** You can pressure cook, slow cook, make rice, make yogurt, steam, sauté, and keep food warm. The inner pot is food-grade stainless steel, and the bottom is tri-ply, which means three layers so heat can spread more evenly.

* **Buttons for common foods.** There are 13 one-touch programs, for things like ribs, soup, beans, rice, poultry, yogurt, and desserts. You still set the food and the liquid.

* **A family size.** It is 6 quarts. It is listed for up to 6 people, and it also suits one person who batch cooks.

* **Cleanup.** They claim the lid, inner pot, and tools are dishwasher safe, and the sides are fingerprint-resistant stainless steel.

* **Safety lines.** They claim more than 10 safety features, plus overheat protection and a locking lid. Quick release still sends out hot steam, so keep your hands clear.

* **Recipes.** Instant Pot offers a free app for iPhone and Android, but you do not have to use it.

## Who it's for

It fits busy parents and anyone who wants dinner without standing at the stove, and it also fits people who meal prep on Sunday.

Skip a crowd of single-use gadgets if this one pot can cover them. It will not cook for a huge party in one go.

* The 6-quart body takes real counter space, though it does replace other pots.
* The first few meals can feel confusing, so read the pressure steps.
* Quick release means hot steam, so do it with care.
* The 70% line is a claim, not a timer for every recipe.

## FAQ

### Can I brown food before pressure cooking?

Yes. There is a sauté mode, so you can brown meat or soften vegetables in the pot, then switch to pressure or slow cook.

### Is the inner pot dishwasher safe?

Yes. They claim the inner pot, lid, and included tools are dishwasher safe.

### How many people does 6 quarts feed?

It is listed for up to 6 people, and it is also fine for batch cooking for one. See the <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B00FLYWNYQ?tag=dailyvirald06-20" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">Instant Pot Duo 6 Qt</a> page for the current model details.

### Does it include recipes?

You can download the free Instant Pot app on iPhone or Android, and the pot also has preset programs if you already know the dish.

## Bottom line

The Duo 6 Qt is a weeknight tool, because one pot can do a faster pressure meal or a slow cook when you have time.

Learn the steam rules before you trust it on a rushed Tuesday. If you only want crispy food, an air fryer is the other path.
