## Quick verdict

You know that moment when your hair is wet, the dryer is in one hand, and a curler is waiting? Switching tools is what makes the morning short. One base that dries and then curls is meant to cut that swap, but you still have to learn the barrels.

The <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0HCF2G9VQ?tag=dailyvirald06-20" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">Shark FlexStyle IonCurl</a> is a dryer and a multi-styler. The claim is the airflow dries and curls without extreme heat. This model is the HD840.


## What it does

* **Dry and style.** One base takes several heads, so you can dry, smooth, add volume, or curl. They claim the air pulls hair around the barrel to make the curl.

* **Ions.** The claim is the ions help hair dry faster and look smoother, with less heat damage.

* **Buttons.** Double-tap AirSet to pause the air, and hold it for a cool shot. Scalp Shield drops to a cooler setting, and they claim that cooler setting protects the scalp from high heat.

* **Noise and practice.** High airflow is loud, and the barrels take a few tries.

## Who it's for

It fits people who already use a dryer and a curling iron, and it fits hair you style most days, from fine to thick.

Skip it if a basic dryer is all you use, because the extra heads will sit in a drawer.

## The good parts

* A dryer and a styler in one.
* Curl barrels that use airflow.
* A cool shot and a cooler scalp mode.
* Fewer tools on the counter.
* A lower-heat claim, if that is what you want.

## Honest limits

* You need practice, because the first curls may look uneven.
* High speed is noisy.
* It is a full system, not one basic dryer, so check today's price.
* "No heat damage" is a claim, not a promise for every head of hair.

## FAQ

### Is it only a curling iron?

No. It dries and styles, so use it as a dryer, then switch heads.

### Will it damage my hair?

No. The claim is the airflow styles without heat damage, and a cooler setting runs lower.

### Is it loud?

Yes, on high. Strong air needs a strong motor, so a quiet tool, this is not.

### How long does it take to learn?

A few practice sessions. The wrap barrels are the new part. See the <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0HCF2G9VQ?tag=dailyvirald06-20" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">Shark FlexStyle IonCurl</a> page for the heads in this kit.

## Bottom line

This Shark is for mornings with too many hot tools, because one base can dry and then curl.

Give yourself practice time. Skip it if you only need a basic dryer, or if fan noise bothers you.
