String floss is still in the drawer, and the morning is already short. A small water flosser can do the between-teeth step in one pass, but it does not replace a dentist.

## What it does

* **Water between teeth.** The claim is many dentists recommend the brand, and that the water-flosser category has earned an ADA seal, but it does not replace a dentist.

* **Two tips, two settings.** You can pick low or high, the grip is non-slip, and they claim it is quiet, but you will still hear water.

* **A small tank.** It holds 6 oz, and they claim the tank is safe on the top rack of the dishwasher. A long session means a refill.

* **Charge it.** A USB cable is in the box, but a wall adapter is not, so use a USB port you already own. A light shows the charge.

* **Shower.** They claim this model is waterproof for the shower, but read the manual anyway.

* **Later costs.** They claim a 2-year limited warranty, and tips wear out and need replacing.

## Who it's for

It fits people who skip string floss, it fits a small bathroom or a travel bag, and it fits a shower routine.

Skip it if you want a huge tank, or if you cannot charge by USB.
