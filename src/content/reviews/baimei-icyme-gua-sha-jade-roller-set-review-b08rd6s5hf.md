## FAQ

### Can I put the entire set in the freezer?
Yes, you can chill or freeze the jade roller and gua sha tools to enhance the cooling effect against puffiness and skin tension.

### Do I need to use an oil or serum with these tools?
It is recommended to use the roller and gua sha with a serum or facial oil. This reduces pulling on the skin and helps support better product absorption.

### Is this set suitable for sensitive skin?
The tools are gentle on all skin types, but you should always apply gentle pressure and avoid using them on broken or irritated skin.

### How do I clean the jade roller and gua sha?
After each use, wipe the tools clean with a soft, damp cloth or mild soap and water, then dry them thoroughly before storing.
