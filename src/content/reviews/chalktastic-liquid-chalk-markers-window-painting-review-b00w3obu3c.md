## Quick verdict

You know that moment when the kids want colour on the window, but you do not want paint that stays until spring? Glass holds the ink because it is smooth, and that is also why a damp cloth can take it off. These markers are meant for art you can erase.

The <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B00W3OBU3C?tag=dailyvirald06-20" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">Chalktastic liquid chalk markers</a> are washable pens for glass and other smooth surfaces, and the set is for temporary art and signs.


## What it does

* **Color that is meant to wipe off.** Use it on clean glass and other non-porous surfaces, because a porous board may not wipe the same way.

* **Two tip sizes.** The tips reverse, so you can make a fine line or a broader stroke.

* **More than one pass.** Bold color on clear glass can need a second coat, and that is normal for this kind of ink.

* **Indoor first.** Rain, freeze, and long outdoor time can fade the art, so this is not vinyl that stays up for weeks.

* **Test a corner.** Tinted film and delicate finishes deserve a small test before you cover them.

## Who it's for

It fits families who want winter window art and kid crafts, and it fits a temporary sign on glass.

Skip it for outdoor lettering that must survive weather, or if you only use dusty stick chalk on a real chalkboard.

## The good parts

* It washes off glass when you are done.
* You can make fine lines or wider strokes.
* A set of colors works for a family project.
* You are not putting permanent paint on a rental window, and you do not need special tools to start.

## Honest limits

* It works best on clean, non-porous surfaces.
* A second pass may be needed for bold color.
* Outdoor rain is not the main job.
* Spot-test delicate glass or film first.

## FAQ

### Will it come off the window?

Yes, on smooth glass. Clean the glass first, then wipe when you are done, but a porous surface may not release the same way.

### Is it paint?

No. It is liquid chalk, so it is for temporary art, not a permanent window job.

### Can it stay outside in the rain?

No. Indoor windows are the easy job, and weather means touch-ups. Read the <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B00W3OBU3C?tag=dailyvirald06-20" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">Chalktastic markers</a> page for the set size you are buying.

### Can kids use them?

Yes, for window art and crafts, with a grown-up nearby. They are washable, but that is not a free pass to draw on every wall.

## Bottom line

If you want winter color on glass without a forever mess, these markers are the simple tool.

Skip them for rain-proof signs or a real chalkboard. For a kitchen project instead, cookie cutters are the other winter activity.
